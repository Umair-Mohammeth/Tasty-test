OUR STORY

We don't do pumpkin spice.

Black Coffin was born in a basement on Raven Alley — one 5kg roaster, a cracked leather couch, and a refusal to serve bad coffee. Now we roast at midnight, brew at dawn, and keep the lights low so the coffee speaks louder.

  • Roasted dark, not burnt — chocolate, cherry, smoke
  • Water is 60% of coffee — we filter to 120 TDS
  • Milk alternative by default — oat is our house milk

2019 — basement roaster. 2021 — first shop. 2024 — midnight roastery behind glass. Same rule: if it's not excellent, we don't serve it.
